# Code of conduct

VidFlow Player is a small project and we want it to stay a friendly one.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, slurs or sexual content, and no personal attacks, in issues, pull
  requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives.
- Keep discussions on topic, and respect a maintainer's decision to close or lock a thread.

If someone breaks these rules, or makes you feel unwelcome, contact the maintainers privately
through the email address on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who keep breaking the
rules.
